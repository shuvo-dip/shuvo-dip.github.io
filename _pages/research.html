Research

My research lies at the interface of soft condensed matter physics, statistical mechanics, and computational modeling, with a focus on biomolecular condensates, nonlinear dynamics, and membrane physics. I employ coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations, polymer physics, and energy landscape theory to understand the material and dynamical properties of soft and active systems.

Research Areas

Biomolecular Condensates

Understanding viscoelasticity, morphology, and phase behavior through sequence-specific energy landscapes and coarse-grained simulations.

Membrane Physics

Exploring how biomembranes interact with proteins, droplets, or confinement boundaries through curvature elasticity and confinement theory.

Nonlinear Dynamics

Studying parametric resonance, instability zones, and pattern formation in driven quantum systems.
